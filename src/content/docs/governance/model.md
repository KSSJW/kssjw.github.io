FuseLeaf Studio currently follows a **single-maintainer model**.

## Maintainer

**KSSJW** is currently responsible for the organization and its projects, including:

* Project development and maintenance
* Technical decisions
* Releases and versioning
* Infrastructure and repositories
* Community management

This structure allows the studio to remain lightweight and make decisions efficiently.

## Contributions

FuseLeaf Studio remains open to community contributions.

Contributors are welcome to submit issues, pull requests, documentation, and other improvements. Contributions are reviewed and merged at the maintainer's discretion.

As the studio grows, its organizational structure may evolve to accommodate additional maintainers and contributors.

## Decision Making

Technical and organizational decisions are currently made by the maintainer.

For significant changes, community feedback and discussion are encouraged whenever appropriate.

> **One maintainer today. An open community always.**
