This policy explains how the names, logos, and identities associated with FuseLeaf Studio may be used.

## FuseLeaf Studio

The name **FuseLeaf Studio**, its logo, and associated visual assets represent the identity of the studio.

You may use them for factual and non-misleading references to FuseLeaf Studio and its official projects.

You may not use them in a way that falsely suggests:

* Official affiliation
* Endorsement or sponsorship
* Ownership or authorship
* Approval by FuseLeaf Studio

Forking an open-source project does not automatically grant permission to use the FuseLeaf Studio name or logo as the primary identity of a derivative project.

## KSSJW

**KSSJW** is the long-term developer identity and online name of the maintainer of FuseLeaf Studio.

You may make factual references to KSSJW and their publicly available work.

You may not:

* Impersonate KSSJW.
* Falsely claim to represent KSSJW.
* Misrepresent KSSJW's authorship or contributions.
* Use the KSSJW identity in a way that falsely suggests endorsement, affiliation, or approval.

## Project Licenses

The licenses of our open-source projects apply to the software and other materials explicitly covered by those licenses.

They do not automatically grant permission to use the **FuseLeaf Studio** name, logo, or the **KSSJW** identity unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Permissions

If you are unsure whether your intended use is permitted, please contact the maintainer.

> **Open source encourages sharing. Identity requires clarity.**
