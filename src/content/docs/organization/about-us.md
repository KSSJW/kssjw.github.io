**FuseLeaf Studio** is a technology-focused open-source team dedicated to building and maintaining software projects.

Our work currently focuses on **Minecraft: Java Edition** and **Flutter**, while remaining open to exploring new technologies and ideas.

We bring independent projects together under a shared identity, making them easier to recognize, discover, and connect with their communities.

We value **technology, openness, collaboration, and fun**.

> **Build things we care about. Share what we learn. Have fun along the way.**
