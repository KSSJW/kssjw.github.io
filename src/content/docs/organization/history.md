FuseLeaf Studio grew from years of ideas, projects, and experimentation.

## Timeline

### 2020 — The Beginning

**KSSJW** began developing the concept for the **Findep project** and its **team structure**, laying the foundation for what would eventually become part of the FuseLeaf Studio story.

### April 3, 2024 — Joined GitHub

KSSJW joined **GitHub**, beginning their journey into public collaborative development and open source.

### October 12, 2025 — First Repository

KSSJW created their first GitHub repository, **Minecart Trains Fork**.

### December 8, 2025 — New Architecture

Starting with version **2.0.0**, Minecart Trains Fork adopted a completely **new architecture**, marking a major change in the project's development.

### December 19, 2025 — WindySky Project

The **Findep Project** evolved into the **WindySky Project**, marking a new stage in its development.

On the same day, the **WindySky Project website** was officially deployed.

### June 30, 2026 — Minecart Trains Fork Becomes Independent

Minecart Trains Fork became **independent** from its original fork network, marking its transition into an independently developed project.

### August 27, 2026 — FuseLeaf Studio

**FuseLeaf Studio** was established as a shared identity for its projects and future development.

### August 28, 2026 — WindySky Joins FuseLeaf Studio

The WindySky Project was **migrated** to FuseLeaf Studio, becoming part of the studio's project ecosystem.

---

**FuseLeaf Studio continues to grow through projects, ideas, and open-source development.**
