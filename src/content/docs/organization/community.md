FuseLeaf Studio is built around an open and welcoming community.

Everyone is welcome to explore our projects, share ideas, report issues, and contribute in their own way.

## Get Involved

You can participate by:

- Sharing ideas and feedback
- Reporting bugs
- Contributing code
- Improving documentation
- Joining discussions
- Helping other community members

You don't need to be a developer or a core contributor to be part of the community.

We value respectful communication, constructive feedback, and a willingness to learn.

> **Everyone has something to contribute.**
