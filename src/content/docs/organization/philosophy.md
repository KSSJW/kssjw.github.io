---
title: Philosophy
description: The principles and values that shape how we build, collaborate, and contribute.
---

We believe good software comes from curiosity, collaboration, and a willingness to keep improving.

Our philosophy is simple:

* **Keep It Simple** — Prefer clear and practical solutions over unnecessary complexity.
* **Stay Curious** — Explore new ideas, technologies, and possibilities.
* **Build Together** — Open source is better when people learn and create together.
* **Share Freely** — Share knowledge, tools, and ideas whenever possible.
* **Have Fun** — Software development should be enjoyable.

We do not expect everything to be perfect. We value learning, experimentation, and continuous improvement.

> **Stay curious. Build together. Keep it fun.**
