Welcome to the **FuseLeaf Studio** documentation.

## Levels

### Organization

Learn about **who we are**, our mission, philosophy, history, and community.

### Projects

Explore **what we build**, including project overviews, usage guides, and project-specific documentation.

### Engineering

Learn **how we build**, including development practices, architecture, coding standards, and technical workflows.

### Governance

Understand **how we work together**, including team structure, responsibilities, decision-making, and policies.

## Where Should I Start?

- **New to FuseLeaf Studio?** Start with [Organization](../../organization/about-us/).
- **Looking for a project?** Browse [Projects](../../projects/windysky/).
- **Contributing code?** See [Engineering](../../engineering/conventions/).
- **Interested in how the team operates?** Read [Governance](../../governance/model/).
- **Reached the end?** Take a look at [Closing](../../closing/acknowledgements/).

> **Build. Explore. Share. Grow.**
